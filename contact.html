Questions

Before you come

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check in from 2:00 pm and check out by 12:00 noon. Ask the front desk for early check-in or late check-out.

Do I pay online?

No. Book here and pay at the front desk when you arrive, by card, bank transfer or cash.

Is there power all day?

Yes. Kiniz runs on its own power backup, so lights, air conditioning and Wi-Fi stay on.

Can I bring children?

Yes. The apartments and penthouse are built for families. The main guest must be 18 or older.

Is there parking?

Free, secure parking inside the gated compound.

Are pets allowed?

Sorry, pets are not allowed.
