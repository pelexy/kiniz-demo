Welcome to Kiniz

Space to live, not just a room to sleep.

Kiniz is two stays in one gated estate: hotel rooms for a quick work trip, and serviced apartments with kitchens and living rooms for families and long visits to Akwa Ibom. Everyone shares the pool, bar, restaurant and 24-hour front desk. Ibom E-Library and Ibom Hall are five minutes away.
